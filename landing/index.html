FAQ

Asked, answered.

How is Zut different from Cursor?

In Cursor the agent writes your files, but npm install, the build and the run all happen on your laptop — a 4GB machine or a phone runs out of road. Zut is the same agentic workflow with the heavy part on our machines (or yours, if you self-host). Same diffs, same review, none of the fan noise.

How is it different from Lovable or Bolt?

They generate an app in their cloud, then hand you a deploy. Zut keeps you in a real IDE: hand-write every line if you want to, review the agent's work as per-file diffs, connect your own Supabase, GitHub and Vercel. You are never locked out of your own code.

Do I need an account?

Yes — an account is how the product works now. Creating one is free, editing is free, and your keys stay in your browser. Your account ties your projects, your agent's home (your machine or ours) and your settings together.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Zut runs entirely in your browser. Optional self-hosting (run broker, opencode on your own machine) unlocks more power for free, but the IDE works without it — that is the point of Zut Compute.

Which AI models can I use?

Any you hold a key for — Gemini, OpenRouter, Claude, ChatGPT — plus anything your self-hosted opencode serves, including free models. Pick per session; type any model ID.

Does it really work on a Chromebook?

Yes. Zero install, no terminal, no build tools on your device. Heavy work runs in the browser tab or on a machine you choose.

Is my code private?

Projects live in your own Supabase project or on your device. API keys never leave your browser except to call the provider you chose.

How do I publish a site?

Connect your Vercel token once in account settings, hit Deploy, and you get a live URL. No anonymous deploys, ever.

What does it cost?

Editing is free. While we're in pilot, agent turns are on us; afterwards you either bring your own key or cover raw compute — never more.
